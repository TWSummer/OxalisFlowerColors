## Boundary decisions

- **Clear requires obvious edges.** [77541853](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/77541853) remains Clear and is now the leading photographic anchor. At the standard 300 px view, the outer edge of the white band must be locatable on at least three petal bases. A pale center alone is insufficient.
- **Faint requires an identifiable weak, localized band.** The owner’s examples [113980346](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/113980346) and [159722286](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/159722286) changed from Absent to Faint. Their Low pigment-variation scores remain.
- **Diffuse inner paling can be unresolved.** [362970560](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/362970560) and [27136204](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/27136204) now have Cannot be determined for both ring and variation. The pale region cannot confidently be isolated as a ring; excluding it could change the variation score. This uncertainty does not automatically establish Faint or High variation.

The [photographic guide](photo-guide.html#ring) and [protocol](protocol.md#white-center-ring) now show those boundaries. No confirmed Absent-ring photograph remains in the reviewed 125; Figure 1 D/F retain that definition. High pigment variation also still lacks a confirmed photographic example in this set. These gaps are stated rather than filled with ambiguous images.
